## What is stored?

The app stores data only locally on your device:

- the names you enter for the players,
- the cards you write yourself,
- which cards you have hidden,
- the current game (whose turn it is, which cards have been played),
- your settings (language, sound, vibration, animations),
- the time you confirmed that you are at least 18 years old.

Answers to questions are never stored. In the group modes (“Never Have I Ever”, “Who’s Most
Likely?”) the app only counts, during a running game, how often someone was tapped – not on
which card. The game data is deleted when the game ends.

The legal basis for storing this data locally is providing the game features you use
(Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Because this data never leaves your device, neither we nor any third
party can access it.

## Sharing, advertising and tracking

No data is transmitted to us or to third parties. The app contains no advertising and no
analytics, crash reporting or tracking services. It does not connect to the internet.

**Sharing a result picture:** After a round you can create a picture of the results and send
it from your device's share menu. The picture is created on your device and goes only to the
app or person you choose there – we do not receive it. Names appear on it only if you turn
them on for that picture (off by default). What happens to a picture you send is up to the
app you hand it to.

When you download the app, Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) process data according
to their own privacy policies. We have no influence on that.

## Permissions

The app does not need access to contacts, location, camera, microphone or notifications.
It cannot read your photos. Only if you save a result picture to your photos from the share
menu does iOS ask once whether the app may add photos.

## Your control

Under **Settings → Data** you can delete all locally stored data at any time. Uninstalling
the app removes it as well.

Because we do not receive any data from you through the app, we cannot provide information
about the app's data either – it exists only on your device. For e-mails you have sent us, you
have the rights of access, rectification and erasure. For privacy questions, contact us at
the address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection
supervisory authority.

## Contact by e-mail

If you write us an e-mail – with “Send feedback” in the app, from the help page of the website
or directly – we process your e-mail address and your message to reply to you (Art. 6(1)(b)
or (f) GDPR). The app only opens your e-mail app and fills in the app version, operating system
and language; you see everything before you send the message and can delete it. We delete your
message once the request has been dealt with and no statutory retention obligation applies.
