SPICY NIGHT is the party game for adults that turns up the heat card by card – from harmless flirting and dating stories to bold confessions.

Add your players, pick a game mode and one of three heat levels – and off you go.

FIVE GAME MODES
• Spicy Questions – questions for the person whose turn it is
• Truth or Dare – everyone chooses for themselves
• Never Have I Ever – the whole group plays along
• Who’s Most Likely? – everyone points at once, with a 3-2-1 countdown
• Random Challenges – small tasks, many with a timer

THREE HEAT LEVELS
🌶️ MILD – Let’s warm up.
🌶️🌶️ HOT – Things are heating up.
🌶️🌶️🌶️ EXTREME – Are you ready? Only if everyone in the group agrees.

FAIR PLAY BUILT IN
• “I pass” is always allowed – nobody has to answer
• No alcohol needed – nobody drinks
• Tasks involving touch only with a clear yes
• Nobody gets humiliated or rated on their looks

WHAT’S INSIDE
• 1560 cards in English and German, no repeats
• Fair player rotation – everyone gets the same number of turns
• 2 to 20 players, switch levels at any time
• Your game is saved, with a summary at the end
• Fully offline – no account, no ads, no tracking

SPICY NIGHT is intended exclusively for adults aged 18 and over.
